The shear panel's 2016 attribution in COMPSII was corrected to 2017 in this
deck after checking the published figure caption. Both simulation panels show
ice precipitation mixing ratio at 7.5 km and 180 minutes; they are not
observations of the dissertation's case study.

Both idealized models use a 50.1 × 50.1 km horizontal domain. In the 2017
figure, the black lines locate source-paper cross sections, not cell motion.
In the 2016b figure, panel (f)'s arrow is the 6.3–7.8 km shear vector, and
the blank panel (i) denotes the absence of GCs in the stable, no-radiation
simulation (ice precipitation mixing ratio does not exceed 0.01 g kg−1).

The Keeler panels use a shared SVG canvas in `sections/opening.md`. Their
original grid borders are aligned by uniform scaling and translation; the
source PNGs, panel data, labels, and aspect ratios are unchanged.

The Keeler figures are AMS-published material, © American Meteorological
Society. Source citations are included on the slide; permissions for further
public redistribution are not established by extraction from the older deck.

The generating-cell definition is quoted from the
[AMS Glossary of Meteorology](https://glossary.ametsoc.org/wiki/Generating_cell/).
